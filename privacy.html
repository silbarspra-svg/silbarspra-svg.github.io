Privacy Policy

SilbarspraApp · Last updated: November 17, 2026

SilbarspraApp is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer and publishes the owner's own videos to their own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the connected channel, read the channel name, and read statistics and analytics (views, likes, comments, watch time) for the channel's videos.
  • TikTok: permission to send videos to the connected account's drafts and read basic profile information and statistics for its videos.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner statistics about them.
  • Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Video files are sent directly from the owner's computer to the platform's official API. They are not stored on any other server.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not use cookies or tracking.

YouTube API Services

SilbarspraApp uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

SilbarspraApp's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google / YouTube: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • TikTok: remove the app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

After access is revoked, the app can no longer reach the account. Locally stored tokens and statistics are deleted when the owner disconnects an account in the app, or on request.
